Good morning Ton, here is your Monday AI briefing.

The big theme this week is the jump from AI that helps you write a query, to AI that runs the whole workflow. Alteryx, Databricks and Snowflake all shipped features that turn data work into deployable agents. And Gartner is now saying that by the end of the year, three quarters of developers will orchestrate rather than write code.

On practical tools for your data teams. Databricks Genie added a managed connection so any agent can ask plain-language questions across your data and get grounded answers. It can also now import a Power BI or Tableau workbook and rebuild it as an AI dashboard. Snowflake meanwhile lets analysts mix AI operations straight into normal SQL, so your SQL-heavy people get AI without leaving familiar ground.

On AI in delivery and consulting, here is the thing worth your attention. Gartner's prediction that most developers will orchestrate rather than code goes right at your fixed-price delivery idea. Fewer hands, more orchestration, and governance becomes the hard part. IBM and Google Cloud also announced a partnership to push AI into production faster, a useful template for how the big firms are packaging AI-powered delivery.

And something to show. Alteryx launched Agent Studio, which lets a business analyst turn an existing data workflow into an autonomous agent, and push it into Slack or Teams. Very demo-friendly if anyone on the team already uses Alteryx.

My pick for this week's session is the Databricks Genie feature that imports a Power BI or Tableau report and turns it into an AI dashboard. It is concrete, it is visual, and someone can drop in a real report and show the before and after in five minutes. That is exactly the "oh, I could use that on Monday" moment these sessions are best at. That's your briefing. Have a good week.
